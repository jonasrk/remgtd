# Step Guide

Status: **accepted** 2026-11-22. One card per step of GTD. Each card has a purpose, a start point, an end point, key questions, advice and a live status.

---

## 1. Capture

**Purpose:** Get everything that has your attention out of your head and into a trusted place.
**Start point:** Stuff is everywhere: in your head, on paper, in apps and messages.
**End point:** Everything is collected in an in-box, and nothing is left nagging you.

**Key questions**
- What's on my mind right now?
- Which in-boxes haven't I emptied yet?

**Advice**
- Capture fast and messily. Clarify is the step where you make sense of it.
- Use as few in-boxes as you can, and empty all of them regularly.

**Live status:** In-box count · Clarify In-boxes checklist ticked / total

## 2. Clarify

**Purpose:** Decide what each piece of Stuff means and what, if anything, you'll do about it.
**Start point:** Unprocessed items in the In-box.
**End point:** The In-box is empty. Every item is trashed, done, or has a home.

**Key questions**
- What is it?
- Is it actionable?
  - **No:** Trash it, move it to Someday/Maybe, or keep it as reference outside Reminders.
  - **Yes:** What's the desired outcome? Does it take more than this one action? If so, it's an Open Loop.
  - What's the next physical, visible action?
- Will it take less than 2 minutes? Then do it now. Is it someone else's to do? Delegate it and add it to Waiting For. Otherwise, defer it.

**Advice**
- Take one item at a time, from the top. Don't put it back, and don't skip it.
- Clarify and Organize happen together. An item leaves the In-box only when it has its final home.
- If an item needs more thinking, the thinking is itself a Next Action, e.g. "Decide whether to …".

**Live status:** In-box count · age of the oldest item

---

## 3. Organize

**Purpose:** Put each clarified item where it belongs, so the system can show it to you at the right moment.
**Start point:** Each item's decision from Clarify.
**End point:** Every item sits in the right list with the right markers, and there are no Violations.

**Key questions**
- Is this an Open Loop with its actions as subtasks, or a standalone Next Action?
- Which Contexts does each Next Action need? (Place/Tool, Agenda, Condition)
- Which action is the Lead Action of this Open Loop?
- Is it Work? Then give Next Actions the Sphere tag (default `#work`), and put everything else in the "Work" section.
- Waiting For: who are you waiting on?

**Advice**
- Write each Next Action so it makes sense on its own, without its Open Loop. Context lists don't show the parent.
- Write each Open Loop's title as the finished outcome ("Passport renewed"), and put details in its notes.
- Give dates only to real deadlines. Appointments belong in the Calendar.

**Live status:** Violations count · the most common Violation

## 4. Reflect

**Purpose:** Keep the system current and complete, so you can trust it.
**Start point:** A system that has drifted during the week.
**End point:** Your mind is clear and every list is current. You are in control and know where you're heading.

**Key questions** (Weekly Review)
- **Get clear:** Are all in-boxes empty, and is everything out of your head?
- **Get current:**
  - Does every Open Loop have a Lead Action?
  - Which Waiting Fors need a follow-up?
  - Is anything coming up in the Calendar that needs a Next Action?
  - Are the Autopilots still on track?
- **Get creative:** Should anything in Someday/Maybe become active now? Anything new to add?

**Advice**
- Do it weekly, and log it wherever you track your reviews.
- A stalled Open Loop is a choice: renegotiate it, or move it to Someday/Maybe (and remove its Contexts).

**Live status:** stalled Open Loops · old Waiting Fors · link to your weekly-review tracker (optional, from config)

---

## 5. Engage

**Purpose:** Choose what to do right now, with confidence.
**Start point:** A current system and a free moment.
**End point:** You're doing the right thing, and you trust that nothing else is being missed.

**Key questions**, in the book's order:
1. **Context:** Where am I, what do I have at hand, and who am I with?
2. **Time available:** How long until my next commitment?
3. **Energy available:** How much focus do I have?
4. **Priority:** Of what's left, what matters most? Start with the Lead Actions.

**Advice**
- At work, open the Work list. The flagged items are your Lead Actions.
- If nothing on the list feels right, the list may not be current. Do a quick Reflect.

**Live status:** Lead Actions in the current Sphere
